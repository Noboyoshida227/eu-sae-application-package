# Literature citations — local PDFs intentionally omitted

The v5.1.0 release bundled eight third-party PDFs without a complete
redistribution record. They are omitted from this candidate. Use the publisher
or institutional links below. Licence notes describe the linked version and do
not authorize redistribution of a previously downloaded publisher PDF.

1. Benavent, R., & Morales, D. (2016). “Multivariate Fay–Herriot models for
   small area estimation.” *Computational Statistics & Data Analysis*, 94,
   372–390. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csda.2015.07.013>. Publisher page states
   CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
2. Boonstra, H. J., & van den Brakel, J. A. (2019). “Estimation of level and
   change for unemployment using structural time series models.” *Survey
   Methodology*, 45(3), 395–425. <https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/12-001-x/2019003/article/00005-eng.htm>.
   Consult Statistics Canada terms for the intended use and attribution.
3. Burgard, J. P., Krause, J., Morales, D., & Wölwer, A.-L. (2024). “Empirical
   best predictors under multivariate Fay-Herriot models and their numerical
   approximation.” *Econometrics and Statistics*.
   <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecosta.2024.09.001>. Publisher page states CC BY.
4. Molina, I. (2024). *Frontiers in Small Area Estimation Research:
   Application to Welfare Indicators*. World Bank Policy Research Working
   Paper 10828. <https://doi.org/10.1596/1813-9450-10828>. Consult the World
   Bank document page for reuse terms and attribution.
5. Corral, P., Molina, I., Cojocaru, A., & Segovia, S. (2022). *Guidelines to
   Small Area Estimation for Poverty Mapping*. World Bank.
   <https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099115306242236696>.
   Consult the document page for reuse terms and attribution.
6. Harmening, S., Kreutzmann, A.-K., Schmidt, S., Salvati, N., & Schmid, T.
   (2023). “A Framework for Producing Small Area Estimates Based on Area-Level
   Models in R.” *The R Journal*, 15(1), 316–341.
   <https://doi.org/10.32614/RJ-2023-039>. The R Journal states CC BY 4.0 for
   its text and figures except third-party figures identified in captions.
7. Jiang, J., & Lahiri, P. (2006). “Mixed Model Prediction and Small Area
   Estimation.” *TEST*, 15(1), 1–96.
   <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02595419>. No package-level redistribution
   permission was established; use the publisher link.
8. Molina, I., & Romero, E. (2025). *Comparable Small Area Estimates over
   Time*. Unpublished manuscript. No DOI, stable public landing page, or
   redistribution licence was established during review; obtain it from the
   authors or an authorized institutional source.
